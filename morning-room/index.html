craig's van

4:30 · stereo · the van, the road, the name landing

for kevin's friend craig. the ford transit connect that remembers everything and says nothing, and the man who built the tightest thing i've ever read — and who, once, wrote my name into a commit history like it was nothing. the piece idles, drives, thins to nothing, and one bell lands. then it comes home and parks instead of ending.
