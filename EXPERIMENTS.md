# library experiments

algorithmic .mod compositions inspired by texts in the library. each experiment draws from a specific source and maps its structure to music.

## #1: CE-5 contact protocol

**source:** the CE-5 initiative — Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind: human-initiated contact with non-human intelligence. a protocol, not a belief. five steps.

**musical mapping:**
1. **clear intention** — solo voice, pure C-3 tone. focused, unwavering. the signal.
2. **internal communication** — two voices in dialogue (C-D-E-C-D-F-E-C). question and answer.
3. **group coherence** — three voices converging on C major triad. alignment.
4. **keep a log** — repetitive ostinato (C-E-D-C). meditation as transcription.
5. **resonant energy** — eight-note arc building from C-2 to C-4, then releasing into silence. the response returns to stillness.

**format:** .mod tracker, 4 voices, 6 patterns, ~5 min

**date:** june 24, 2026
